Third-party material
--------------------

Zwanenburg, C., Wittekoek, B., Martinelli, M., Alderlieste, E., & Zwaan, R. (2023).
Calibration chamber for numerical tools [data set]. Zenodo. doi:10.5281/zenodo.8066899.
Licensed CC BY 4.0. data/clay_laboratory.json contains net bearing pressures derived
from these measurements (contact detection and buoyancy correction as described in the
report). The processing is ours; the measurements are the authors'.

Li, C., Zhang, T., & Goldman, D. I. (2013). A terradynamics of legged locomotion on
granular media. Science, 339(6126), 1408-1412. doi:10.1126/science.1229163.
The measured stress-per-depth slopes of the article's additional data table S5 are shown
in figures for comparison and cited; they are not redistributed in data/.

Ruck, J., Pradeep, S., Bush, J., Sigg, E., Qian, F., & Jerolmack, D. (2025). Unified granular
intrusion dynamics for planetary materials. EarthArXiv, doi:10.31223/X5246P. Data and code: Zenodo,
doi:10.5281/zenodo.15610745, licensed CC BY 4.0. data/rod_intrusion.json contains force-depth slopes
derived from these measurements (processing described in the report, Appendix A2).

Douglas, M. M., Colicci, V., Sandu, N. A., Yilmaz, L., Kamrin, K., & Perron, J. T. (2026). Effects of root
architecture on plant anchoring in noncohesive sediment. JGR Earth Surface 131(8), e2025JF008680.
Data: Harvard Dataverse, doi:10.7910/DVN/VXRLSK, CC0 1.0. data/rod_pullout.json contains the
published peak forces.

Mishra, A. K., Tramacere, F., Guarino, R., Pugno, N. M., & Mazzolai, B. (2018). A study on plant root
apex morphology as a model for soft robots moving in soil. PLOS ONE 13(6), e0197411. Data: Zenodo,
doi:10.5281/zenodo.1211631, licensed CC BY 4.0. data/probe_penetration.json contains mean forces
derived from these measurements (Appendix A4).

Kruger, R. (2025). Multi-scale Cullinan sand (fine sand) dataset. Zenodo, doi:10.5281/zenodo.17584463,
licensed CC BY 4.0; protocol in Kruger, Els & Hamersma (2023), J. Terramechanics 109:45-62.
data/plate_sinkage.json contains mean pressure-sinkage curves derived from these measurements
(Appendix A5).
