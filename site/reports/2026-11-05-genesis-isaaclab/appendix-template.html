Methods and measurements

__RUN_COUNT__ displayed executions: 72 rollouts and 36 learning runs. Three seeds per backend and workload.

Timing contract

MeasurementIncluded work
RolloutFour 5 ms simulator steps per transition, actions, observations, rewards, terminations and resets. RGB adds native rendering. 500 measured transitions after 50 warmup transitions; batches 256, 4,096 or 8,192 for state and 64 for RGB.
StartupRuntime imports through first usable observation for that rollout workload. Python/site initialization is outside the clock. Fresh workers reuse disk/JIT caches.
LearningShared PPO, 512 environments, 500 iterations × 24 rollout steps = 6.144M training transitions. Training includes initial/periodic evaluation and excludes model/optimizer construction, simulator startup and checkpoint saving.

ANYmal-C tracks forward velocity, Cartpole balances, and Franka reaches seeded targets near home with open fixed fingers. RGB augments full proprioception. Both engines receive the same prepared robot inputs, controllers, task and PPO code.

Held-out deterministic policy evaluation lasts 5 s and is sampled every 50 iterations. A target requires survival ≥0.95 plus mean error ≤0.25 m/s (ANYmal), ≤0.1 rad (Cartpole), or ≤0.05 m (Franka). Franka survival is valid joint state. Error averages include steps after automatic failure resets. First-target times are conditional on reached seeds; misses are censored at the fixed budget.

Genesis uses Newton with a 25-iteration maximum and native tolerance. Isaac Lab MJWarp uses Newton/implicitfast, 100 solver and 50 line-search iterations, tolerance 1e-6, pyramidal friction, one substep and CUDA graphs. These settings do not establish equal physical accuracy. Native renderers differ in image quality.
