Franka cable pickup: four learning backends

Eight fresh Franka cable runs compare stock RSL-RL PPO, native CUDA PPO, official Torch FlashSAC AMP and native CUDA FlashSAC AMP. FlashSAC on Cartpole: a replay capacity pilot

With pristine authors' Torch FlashSAC, the replay buffer with 10 million transitions passed the balancing gate in 2/2 seeds at 1,000 iterations and at both 900 and 1,000. The 131,072-transition buffer passed in 0/2. Only replay capacity changed. This pilot with two seeds and 1,000 iterations does not establish stability during longer training.

  • 10,000,000 transitions

Cartpole learning with stock and native CUDA learners

Native CUDA PPO used 40.7% and 43.5% less full training-process time than stock RSL-RL PPO across two matching GPU allocations. Both PPO recipes passed the sustained balancing gate in 3/4 seeds on each allocation. Native FlashSAC used 47.5% and 48.3% less full training-process time than the authors' Torch AMP, but neither FlashSAC recipe met the balancing gate at any saved checkpoint.
